# About us

Example Plumbing Co. is a family-run plumbing and heating business serving Bristol and the surrounding area since 2004.

# Services

We fix leaks, install and service boilers, unblock drains, and fit bathrooms. We do not do gas safety certificates for landlords.

# Opening hours

We are open 8am to 6pm Monday to Friday, and 9am to 1pm on Saturday. Emergency call-outs are available 24/7.

# Pricing

We give free written quotes after a site visit. We never quote prices over chat.

# Areas covered

Bristol, Bath, Keynsham, Portishead and Clevedon.
